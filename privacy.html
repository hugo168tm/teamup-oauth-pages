隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date：2026 年 10 月 9 日

This Privacy Policy explains how the "TeamUp 的士預約助手" (TeamUp Taxi Booking Assistant) application handles personal data. It applies solely to this application, which is used privately by the fleet operator.

1. 資料收集 / Information We Collect

本應用程式只會處理車隊營運者（即使用者本人）主動輸入嘅的士預約資料，包括：

  • 乘客聯絡人姓氏（例如：陳先生／李小姐）
  • 乘客或聯絡人電話號碼
  • 上車地點、目的地
  • 預約日期及時間、回程時間
  • 輪椅／隨行人數、行李數目、航班編號等預約備註

This application only processes taxi booking data actively entered by the fleet operator (the user themself), including: contact surname, phone number, pickup location, destination, booking date/time, return time, wheelchair/companion counts, luggage, flight number and other booking remarks.

2. 資料用途 / How We Use Information

上述資料只用於以下用途：

  • 將預約寫入車隊嘅 TeamUp 日曆，以便車隊調度車輛；
  • 將乘客電話同步至車隊營運者本人嘅 Google 通訊錄（電話簿），方便聯絡乘客確認行程。

The data is used only to: (a) write bookings into the fleet's TeamUp calendar for vehicle scheduling; and (b) sync passenger phone numbers into the operator's own Google Contacts for trip coordination.

3. 資料分享 / Information Sharing

本應用程式唔會向任何第三方出售、出租、轉讓或公開個人資料。資料只會儲存喺車隊營運者自己嘅 Google 帳戶及 TeamUp 日曆內。Google 及 TeamUp 作為雲端服務提供者，會按其各自嘅服務條款處理儲存於其平台嘅資料。

We do not sell, rent, transfer or disclose personal data to any third party. Data is stored only in the operator's own Google account and TeamUp calendar. Google and TeamUp, as cloud service providers, process data stored on their platforms under their respective terms of service.

4. 資料保留及刪除 / Data Retention and Deletion

預約資料會按車隊營運者嘅營運需要保留。車隊營運者可隨時喺 Google 通訊錄或 TeamUp 日曆中刪除任何聯絡人或預約記錄；刪除後相關資料即無法再透過本應用程式存取。

Booking data is retained as long as needed for the operator's operations. The operator may delete any contact or booking record at any time in Google Contacts or the TeamUp calendar; once deleted, the data can no longer be accessed through this application.

5. 資料安全 / Data Security

本應用程式透過 Google OAuth 授權存取，應用程式本身唔會儲存任何密碼。通訊錄同步僅限於車隊營運者本人嘅 Google 帳戶，並受 Google 帳戶安全機制保護。

Access is authorized via Google OAuth; the application never stores any passwords. Contact sync is limited to the operator's own Google account and is protected by Google's account security mechanisms.

6. 未成年人資料 / Children's Privacy

本應用程式唔會故意收集未滿 18 歲人士嘅個人資料；所有預約資料均由車隊營運者輸入及管理。

This application does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18; all booking data is entered and managed by the fleet operator.

7. 政策更新 / Changes to This Policy

如有需要，本政策可能會更新。更新後嘅版本會刊登喺同一網址，並標示新嘅生效日期。

This policy may be updated when necessary. The updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective date.
